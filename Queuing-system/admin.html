Creative specifications for the side terminals
Recommended flyer canvas: 1080 x 2291 px portrait (DL ratio, 99 x 210 mm). Each terminal uses one reusable ad container: the top 75% is reserved for the flyer/video and the bottom 25% for the QR code. Keep faces, logos, and important wording inside the centre 80% safe area. Use PNG/JPEG/WEBP for stills and MP4/WebM for video; use high-contrast artwork and avoid placing text close to the crop edges. QR codes should be generated from a short URL and remain dark on a white background for reliable scanning from reception distance. Maximum upload size: 40MB images, 80MB videos. Images show for their configured duration; videos remain visible until playback completes.
